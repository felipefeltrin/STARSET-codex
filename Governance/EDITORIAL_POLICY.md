## Purpose

This document is the single source of truth for editorial authority, canon precedence, contradiction handling, interpretation, and dispute resolution within **The STARSET Codex**.

## Scope

This policy applies to lore chapters, appendices, registries, captions, maps, diagrams, archive entries, interviews, demonstrations, ARG records, and editorial notes.

## Editorial Principles

### Document First. Interpret Second.

Editors SHALL state what official sources establish before offering interpretation.

### Canon Before Speculation

Evidence SHALL take precedence over preferred interpretations. Speculative material may be included only when clearly labeled according to [`../Specifications/CANON_CONFIDENCE.md`](../Specifications/CANON_CONFIDENCE.md).

### No Lore Left Behind

Conflicting, superseded, or retired lore SHALL be preserved when historically relevant. Editors must document the older claim, the newer claim, the conflict, and the current editorial conclusion.

**Example:** If a comic adaptation shortens or changes a novel event, the Codex records both versions and identifies the novel as the higher-priority source.

## Canon Hierarchy

### Tier I — Primary Canon

Highest authority:

- *The Prox Transmissions*.
- *A Brief History of the Future*.
- Future official prose works explicitly presented as primary narrative canon.

### Tier II — Official Narrative Media

Canon unless contradicted by Tier I:

- Albums and official narrative material accompanying them.
- Official music videos with identifiable narrative content.
- Demonstrations.
- Official ARG material.
- Official STARSET websites and communications that advance the story.

### Tier III — Adaptations

Canon where compatible with higher tiers:

- The Marvel comic adaptation.
- Future adaptations of primary works.

Adaptation-specific additions must not override the source work unless an official statement explicitly revises canon.

### Tier IV — Creator Commentary

Statements by Dustin Bates or other authorized creators may clarify canon but do not automatically supersede published narrative works.

### Tier V — Community Reconstruction

Community analysis may connect evidence but must always be labeled as reconstruction or theory. It cannot establish canon by repetition or popularity.

## Resolving Contradictions

Editors SHALL use this process:

`Identify all relevant sources -> Assign each source its canon tier -> Describe the contradiction neutrally -> Apply precedence -> Record unresolved uncertainty -> Preserve all historically relevant versions`

No contradictory claim may be silently deleted merely because a newer source exists.

## Theories and Reconstructions

A theory or reconstruction SHALL:

- Be explicitly labeled.
- Identify its supporting official evidence.
- State what remains unconfirmed.
- Use the lowest confidence level honestly supported by the evidence.
- Never be phrased as established fact.

## Historical Figures

When a historical person appears in STARSET lore, the Codex SHALL distinguish:

- Verified historical information.
- Fictionalized actions or affiliations introduced by STARSET.
- Points where the narrative diverges from documented history.

**Example:** Nikola Tesla and Wardenclyffe Tower are historical; Teslonian involvement with future transmissions is part of the fictional narrative.

## Editorial Authority

Routine editorial decisions are resolved through the review workflow. Maintainers may approve wording, evidence classification, and structural changes.

Canon disputes SHALL be resolved through documented evidence and this policy, not seniority or personal preference. When evidence remains insufficient, the disputed point must remain explicitly unresolved.

Major changes to the canon hierarchy or project principles require:

1. A documented architectural or editorial decision.
2. Maintainer review.
3. A versioned governance update.
4. A changelog entry.

## Immutable Releases

Published releases SHALL remain unchanged. Corrections require a new release version and changelog entry.
