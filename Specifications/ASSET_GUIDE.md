## Purpose

This document defines storage, format, naming, metadata, attribution, and revision standards for non-Markdown assets.

## Asset Categories

### FIG — Figures

Use for:

- Photographs.
- Screenshots.
- Illustrations.
- Artwork.
- Recreated in-universe documents.

Preferred formats:

- SVG for original vector illustration.
- PNG for lossless raster images and screenshots.
- JPG or JPEG for photographs where lossless storage is unnecessary.

### DIG — Diagrams

Use for:

- Timelines and timeline branches.
- Organization charts.
- Relationship graphs.
- Process diagrams.
- Technology schematics.

Preferred format: SVG. PNG is an acceptable fallback when SVG is impractical.

### MAP — Maps

Use for:

- Real-world event maps.
- Fictional political maps.
- Site maps.
- Astronomical maps.
- PROX reconstructions.

Preferred format: SVG. High-resolution PNG may be used for complex raster cartography.

## Directory Structure

Assets SHOULD be stored within the package that owns them:

```text
assets/
├── figures/
├── diagrams/
└── maps/
```

Shared assets MAY be stored in a future repository-level shared asset directory when multiple packages depend on the same file.

## Filenames

The permanent identifier SHALL be the filename stem.

**Examples:**

- `FIG-0012.png`
- `DIG-0004.svg`
- `MAP-0003.svg`

Do not append mutable titles to filenames. Titles belong in metadata.

## Companion Metadata

Every asset SHALL have an external companion YAML file.

**Example:**

```text
DIG-0004.svg
DIG-0004.metadata.yaml
```

Required fields are defined in [`METADATA_SPEC.md`](METADATA_SPEC.md).

## Captions

Every published asset SHALL have a caption containing:

- Identifier.
- Human-readable title.
- Brief explanatory description when necessary.
- Confidence label when reconstructed or speculative.

**Example:**

> **DIG-0004 — Proposed branching structure of the altered timelines.** Reasonable Reconstruction.

## Resolution and Legibility

- Raster assets SHOULD be large enough for print at intended dimensions.
- Diagrams and maps SHALL remain legible in both standalone chapter PDFs and the compiled Codex.
- Text embedded in assets SHOULD be minimized when equivalent labels can be generated by the output system.
- Assets SHOULD avoid information conveyed solely through color.

## Revision

An asset keeps the same ID when revised without changing its conceptual identity. Metadata revision is incremented and the changelog records significant changes.

A materially different asset with a different purpose requires a new ID.

**Example:** Correcting two mislabeled nodes keeps `DIG-0004`; replacing it with a different diagram representing another model requires a new `DIG` ID.

## Source, Licensing, and Attribution

Every asset SHALL record:

- Creator.
- Original source.
- Copyright or license status.
- Required attribution.
- Whether the asset is original, adapted, reproduced, or reconstructed.

Unlicensed copyrighted media may not be included merely because it is available online. The public repository must have a lawful basis for every distributed asset.
